## Description:

Use when user asks to "generate workshop outline", "create training agenda", "design course structure", "build workshop schedule", or requests help planning training sessions. Applies MECE structure, TfBR design (4Cs), and VAK-inclusive learning.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

Employees, external facilitators, and training designers use this skill to create learner-centered workshop outlines, course agendas, and training session structures. It emphasizes MECE module design, TfBR 4C flow, VAK-inclusive activities, action-based learning outcomes, and practical materials planning.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Generated workshop outlines may carry the skill's default facilitation assumptions even when a different learning design method is needed.

Mitigation: Specify the preferred learning model, audience constraints, and required format, then review the agenda before use.

Risk: Workshop guidance may be incomplete or mismatched if the user omits audience, duration, format, outcomes, or practical constraints.

Mitigation: Provide the missing requirements or ask the agent to clarify them before generating the final outline.

Risk: The included example is in Chinese, which may influence adaptation or reuse when another language is expected.

Mitigation: Ask explicitly for the desired output language and localize examples, materials, and activities for the target audience.
