## Description:

Multi-platform content generator for Chinese social media that produces publish-ready posts for 小红书, 知乎, 公众号, and 抖音 with platform-native formatting, hooks, and CTAs.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Use Case:

External users and content creators use this skill to draft Chinese social-media posts, articles, answers, and short-video scripts with platform-specific structure, hooks, hashtags, and calls to action.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: The skill may activate on general writing prompts rather than only explicit Chinese social-media platform requests.

Mitigation: Confirm the topic, target platform, style, word count, and image needs before drafting, or narrow the activation wording before deployment.

Risk: Generated social-media copy can become misleading if it includes unverifiable data, fake reviews, or absolute claims.

Mitigation: Review factual claims and citations before publication, and keep the artifact guidance to avoid fake experiences and absolute claims.
