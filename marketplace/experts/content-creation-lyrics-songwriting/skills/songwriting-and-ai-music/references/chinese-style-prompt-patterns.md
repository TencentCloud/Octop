# Chinese-Style Suno Prompt Patterns

> A worked example of Chinese-style Suno custom mode packaging through iterative
> refinement. This song, "手谈" (Hand Talk), draws inspiration from Go (围棋) imagery
> and philosophical dialogue. It demonstrates how a single set of lyrics can evolve
> through three distinct production directions: solo female, expanded solo, and duet.
>
> Use this as a concrete reference for structuring your own Chinese-style prompt
> packages and planning iteration cycles.

---

## The Song: 手谈

Go is called "hand talk" (手谈) in Chinese — playing with hands instead of speaking.
The concept maps the dialogue between a mortal (black stones) and the divine (white stones)
onto a Go board, using the game as a metaphor for existence and understanding.

## Version 1: Dramatic Female (初始版)

The first production direction chosen for this lyric set.

### Style Prompt

```
Chinese traditional opera dramatic ballad, pentatonic scale, guqin dominant.
A female voice with theatrical presence — starts intimate and controlled,
builds to soaring, devastating power at the chorus.
Spoken word verses, belted emotional climax at chorus.
Dramatic contrast: whisper vs surge, sparse vs full.
Echoing reverb on vocal, like calling across a mountain valley.
Second vocal harmony enters at bridge, like two voices from opposite shores.
Chinese percussion accents at key moments — a single drum hit, a bell.
Guqin arpeggios, erhu subtle texture beneath the voice.
BPM 52, pentatonic minor.
Dynamic journey: intimate → rising → powerful → poignant → fade.
No western instruments. No piano. Pure Chinese timbre.
```

song is inherently a dialogue. The two speakers naturally split: male = human
(the questioner), female = divine (the answerer).

### Style Prompt

```
Chinese traditional opera dramatic ballad, pentatonic scale, guqin dominant.
Male-female duet — two voices in dialogue.
Male voice: earthly, searching, vulnerable, restrained at first,
grows to raw desperate questioning.
Female voice: otherworldly, steady, knowing, serene power —
starts warm and intimate, builds to devastating emotional peak.
Spoken word verses, belted climaxes.
Dramatic contrast: whisper vs surge, sparse vs full.
Voices intertwine in bridge, call and response throughout.
Guqin arpeggios, erhu texture, Chinese percussion accents.
BPM 52, pentatonic minor.
Dynamic arc: intimate dialogue → rising tension → desperate plea
→ revelation → quiet surrender.
No western instruments. No piano. Pure Chinese timbre.
```

## What This Pattern Teaches

### About the Iterative Process

The song went through three distinct versions driven by iterative direction:

| # | Direction | Changed |
|---|-----------|---------|
| Initial | First draft | — |
| V1 | "不够中国风 → 女声，戏剧风" | Style prompt + metatags only |
| V2 | Expanded, more dramatic | Lyrics expanded (chorus +13 lines, bridge rewritten) |
| V3 | Duet version | Voice split across existing lyrics, minimal text changes |

Key lesson: **Direct performance and format, not words.** Once lyrics are confirmed,
preserve them unless explicitly asked to rewrite. Lyrics integrity matters.

### About Structure (all versions)

1. **Lyrics-led structure** — the lyrics were written FIRST, then style + metatags
   built around them. Don't reverse-engineer lyrics to fit a genre; write the
   words and find the genre that serves them.

2. **Contrast is the engine** — the power of the chorus comes from the quiet that
   precedes it. The dynamic arc (静→起→放→收→寂) is built into the metatags.

3. **Empty space is an instrument** — "[Silence]" before the final line forces
   the listener to wait. In a 3-minute song, a 3-second silence is an eternity.
   Use it sparingly for maximum impact.

4. **Vocal persona > gender** — "dramatic female voice with theatrical presence
   that starts intimate and builds to devastating power" is more specific than
   just "[Female Vocals]". Suno uses this to shape performance, not just timbre.

5. **Negative space in style field** — `No western instruments. No piano.` is as
   important as the positive instructions. Suno defaults to piano if you don't
   block it.

6. **Structural metatags define the journey** — without [Verse 1], [Chorus],
   [Bridge], [Outro], Suno defaults to flat verse/chorus repetition. Every
   section gets a tag and a performance direction.

7. **Duets need role clarity** — assign each voice a character (not just a gender)
   so Suno can differentiate them. Call-and-response sections (Verse 3) produce
   the most convincing duet separation.

## Alternate Vocal Personas (untested variations)

For future sessions adapting these lyrics to different styles:

**Low male spoken-word** (original concept V0):
```
Chinese minimalism, ambient folk, meditative, sparse.
A low male voice, intimate and weathered, spoken-word delivery,
never rushed — every word placed like a stone on a board.
Guqin or muted piano, single notes, wide spaces.
BPM 55, D minor or pentatonic scale.
No percussion. No bass. No climax. Just presence.
```

**Ethereal female** (alternative):
```
Chinese ambient, ethereal, meditative.
A floating female voice, breathy, like mist over water.
Guqin harmonics, zheng arpeggios, sparse.
Pentatonic scale. BPM 50.
Like a temple at dawn. No hurry.
```
